## Findings

### 1. MAJOR — §78.7 "The route", §75.2 update, §78.4 row 1: the inference from Lemma 78.C to "the route is dead (§75.1)" is not justified as written

The text reads: "`dim P(c) ≥ … ≥ D^{(1/3)(n−o(n))}/2`. That is a `D^α` with `α = 1/3`, and §75.1 shows any `D^α`, `α > 0`, gives no bound through [ASU12] Thm. 2.6." There are two gaps.

**(i) The `o(n)` depends on `D`.** `Q̰` is a supremum (p. 7), so `Q(q^{⊠n})` at a fixed `n` can be far below `D^{n/3}`.

- §75.1's (★) evaluates the method at `D = s·m`, `n = s`, with `D ≫ n`.
- A fixed-`D`, `n → ∞` statement says nothing in that regime.
- §75.1 costs *upper* bounds `B(D)^s` on `V_3`. Lemma 78.C is a *lower* bound on the method's output, which is a different statement.

**(ii) `c` may depend on `n` (and on `D`).** Lemma 78.C bounds `liminf_n` for each *fixed* `c`. A tester family `q_{c_n}^{⊗n}` is not covered by the statement of Thm 1.8, because the convergence of `Q(·)^{1/n}` to `Q̰` is not uniform in the theorem as stated.

**What does survive.** The method cannot prove a bound of ASU Conjecture 3's shape, `V_3(D,n) ≤ C·b^n` uniformly in `D` and `n`. Uniformity in `c` also holds, but it comes from the *proof*, not the statement:

- Thm 2.1 and Lemma 2.8 give one-shot restrictions, and Lemma 2.12 (p. 20) gives `q^{⊠3} ≥ ⟨Q₂,Q₃,Q₁⟩`.
- These are matrix-multiplication tensors with `min(ab,bc,ca) ≥ D` (Thm 2.1).
- For fixed `D` they range over finitely many triples `(a,b,c)`.
- So `Q(q_c^{⊠3k}) ≥ min over those triples of Q(⟨a,b,c⟩^{⊠k}) = D^{k−o(k)}`, uniformly in `c`. This is valid over fields with `|F| > D`. For smaller fields, pass to `F̄` (finding 8).

Whether the method fails in the colour-coding regime `D = s·m` is NOT settled. It would need a one-shot subrank bound for rectangular matrix multiplication; that is PLAUSIBLE, not read.

The same fixed-`D` gap was already present in §75.2's `Λ` branch. The change repeats it, and its "§75.1 shows" makes it explicit.

**Fix**, replacing the first bullet of "What it changes" in §78.7:

> * **The route.** By Lemma 75.B, `dim P(c) ≥ slice-rank(T_c)/2`, and by 78.C `liminf_n slice-rank(T_c)^{1/n} ≥ D^{1/3}` for every fixed `c`; the proof of [BCLSZ25] Thm. 1.8 (Lemmas 2.8, 2.12, 2.13 and Thm. 2.1, pp. 19–20) makes this uniform in `c` at fixed `D`, because `q_c^{⊠3}` restricts to one of finitely many matrix-multiplication tensors `⟨a,b,c⟩` with `min(ab,bc,ca) ≥ D`. **So no product tester, with `c` allowed to vary with `n`, can give a bound of [ASU12] Conjecture 3's shape (`b^n` uniform in `D`), in any characteristic** — the first branch of §75.2's verdict *for product testers*, reached without `Λ`. This is a fixed-`D`, `n → ∞` statement: it does not bound the method's output in §75.1's colour-coding regime `D = s·m`, `n = s` (that would need one-shot subrank bounds; not attempted). The same caveat applies to §75.2's `Λ` branch.

Make the matching edit in the §75.2 update: "the route is dead whatever `Λ` does" becomes "no product tester gives a `D`-uniform `b^n` bound, whatever `Λ` does". In §78.4 row 1, change "so the route cannot remove `D` (§75.1)" to "so no product tester gives a `D`-uniform bound (ASU Conj. 3 shape)".
